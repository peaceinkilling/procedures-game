# Issue Procedure Source Map

## Authority used

- `doc 2 main remove/RAOS Part II.pdf`, revised edition 1986: Chapter 5, especially paras 164–191 (PDF pages 79–86).
- `doc 2 main remove/DGOS/DGOS TI 002 ISSUE PROCEDURE NORMAL.pdf`, Issue 3/reprint dated October 2005: broad principles paras 2–16 and office detail through para 224 (PDF pages 6–55).

The duplicate RAOS Part II files under `RAOS/` and `DGOS/` are library copies; citations use the root copy consistently. Training plans and slides are secondary aids and do not override these two sources.

## Verified normal-flow facts

- The demand enters through HQ, is sorted at ISS, visits ULC only for a non-static unit, is checked, controlled and converted into normally six Issue Voucher copies. RAOS para 190(a–g); DGOSTI-002 paras 2–7, 20–89.
- Selection sends IV3 and IV4 to SDIC, while IV1, IV2, IV5 and IV6 accompany stores to Packing. RAOS para 190(l); DGOSTI-002 paras 11, 95, 128.
- Packing puts IV2 in Package No. 1, creates one original and one duplicate Packing Note per package, sends IV5 back to SDIC, and passes IV1/IV6 plus packages to Traffic using Packing Completion Advice. RAOS para 190(m); DGOSTI-002 paras 141–156.
- The Packing Note original is enclosed in its corresponding package; its serial is cross-referenced on IV1, IV5 and IV6. The duplicate remains in the bound Packing record. DGOSTI-002 paras 142, 148, 152–153.
- Rail, road, postal and local-collection missions are modeled as explicitly named document sets rather than as invented single forms. Convoy Note copies and return control follow paras 170–180; rail RR/PWB and Military Credit Note handling follows paras 163–175; postal register/receipt handling follows paras 182–185; local issue authority, signature and return-to-stock controls follow paras 187–192.
- Package-collection acknowledgement and Packing Completion Advice hand-over evidence follow DGOSTI-002 paras 155–161.
- CAB posts from IV4 and files it; IV3 is scheduled to LAO. RAOS para 190(o–p); DGOSTI-002 paras 194–205.
- IV6 receives despatch details, passes through S&M for Issue Time Check, and is filed with the indent in the unit pad. DGOSTI-002 paras 174–177, 213(e–g), 219–224.

## Source-qualified IV2/IV5 ruling

The completed game uses IV2 as the returned receipted acknowledgement and keeps IV5 on the packing-progress/LAO route. This ruling is supported by:

- RAOS Part II para 190(m) and (q): IV2 is enclosed in Package No. 1 and No. 2 copies are filed in control-number order; IV5 is scheduled to LAO.
- DGOSTI-002 broad principles paras 12–16 and its detailed IV5 progress/scheduling instructions in paras 96, 154, 204 and 213(d–j).
- The supplied 2022 Issue Procedure deck, slides 60 and 62–64: IV2 is in Package No. 1, its return is progressed on the IRPS, and IV5 is held for LAO.

DGOSTI-002 para 213(n) alone says “No 5” for the returned receipted copy. That line conflicts with the same instruction’s IV5-to-LAO lifecycle and with the other supplied sources. The anomaly remains disclosed in each affected character’s review note and in automated tests; it is not silently removed.

## Issues Control Sheet roster clarification

The roster’s “Issues Control Registration Sheet / equivalent control medium” is implemented as the IRPS control-medium mission, not as an invented third sheet. DGOSTI-002 paras 28, 51, 66–70 and 210–214 establish the two-copy IRPS lifecycle. The supplied deck slides 43, 49–50 and 81 explicitly label the form “Issue Registration Progress Sheet”, show its columns and assign the original to R&PS/CRS while the duplicate follows Sub-Depot execution.
