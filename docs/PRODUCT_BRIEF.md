# Product Brief

## Product idea
A top-down depot adventure in which the learner becomes a document, copy, register, transit paper, or physical consignment and must navigate the correct offices while completing office-specific mini-games.

## Core loop
1. Select or receive a character/entity.
2. Read the current state and mission clue.
3. Navigate to the correct next office.
4. Complete that office's action.
5. See the entity change state through stamps, endorsements, signatures, packing, posting, or filing.
6. Continue until the entity reaches its final disposal.
7. Reconstruct the full route from memory.

## Modes
- Guided campaign: full prompts and highlighted office.
- Arcade: timed route and combo scoring.
- Exam: no compass or highlighted target.
- Character campaign: one complete document lifecycle.
- Flow reconstruction: drag offices into sequence after completing a campaign.
- Scenario mode: static/non-static unit, rail/road/post/local issue, NA item, dues-out, partial issue, discrepancy, missing document, etc.

## UX principles
- Never cover the depot map with instruction overlays.
- Show one immediate objective, but keep full route progress accessible.
- Represent every copy by a distinctive shape/icon and persistent lifecycle state.
- Explanations must say what moved, why, what changed, who now holds it, and what waits elsewhere simultaneously.
