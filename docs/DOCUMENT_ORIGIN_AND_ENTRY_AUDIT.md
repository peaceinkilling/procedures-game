# Document Origin and Entry Audit

This audit records the branch that creates each playable document family and the entries added inside the depot. It distinguishes a document's **originator** from later offices that add control, progress, custody or posting evidence.

Primary references are the local copies of RAOS Part II, DGOSTI-002 Issue Procedure Normal and DGOSTI-001 Receipt Procedure Normal.

## Review boundary

- Physical stores and packages are not presented as forms. Their displayed values are custody, quantity, condition and package-marking evidence.
- DGOSTI assigns preparation/control of transit documents to Traffic, but the exact field layout varies by carrier or convoy form. The game therefore teaches the supported route and references without inventing a universal layout.
- The correction made in this audit is that the Schedule of Indents accompanies the originating demand. DGOSTI-002 says ISS receives it with the demands; it does not say ISS creates it.
