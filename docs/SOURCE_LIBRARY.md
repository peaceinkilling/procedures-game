# Source Library Routing

The `doc 2 main remove` folder is treated as a reference library, not as one undifferentiated authority.

## Active primary sources

- RAOS Part II: common accounting, Receipt and Issue rules.
- DGOSTI-001 / DGOS TI 001 PDF: normal Receipt Procedure.
- DGOSTI-002 / DGOS TI 002 PDF: normal Issue Procedure.

## Active secondary sources

- `Issue Procedure/Issue Procedure teaching plan.doc`
- `Issue Procedure/ISSUE PROCDEURE Amdt by Maj HS Bisht 10 Nov 22.pptx`
- Receipt teaching-plan DOCX and the two Receipt PPTX files under `Receipt Procedure/`

These may clarify teaching language but cannot override RAOS or the applicable DGOSTI.

## Indexed but outside the current normal Issue/Receipt campaign

- RAOS Part I and AOC Officers Handbook.
- DGOSTI/DGOS instructions for stocktaking, dues-out control, storage-location records, dispersal, RSSD, salvage, provisioning, repair, payment/book-debit/local purchase, equipment holding, vehicle depots, ammunition depots, supply dropping and other specialist processes.
- `Spl to Corps (AOC).pdf` and DGOS TI 015/TRU-accounting references.

These files stay available for later specialist campaigns. They do not supply missing normal-route facts by analogy, and vehicle/ammunition procedures are not mixed into normal-depot Issue or Receipt because both primary instructions expressly exclude those depot types from the normal procedure.
